`ThemeScope`: when the scope's mode changes, every transition inside it (its portalled dialogs, menus and toasts included) is held still for the frame of the change, so text that transitions its colour, such as `NavMenu`'s links, `Segmented` and the icon buttons, flips in the same frame as the surface behind it. Before, that text faded over `--motion-duration-1` (about 120 ms) from its old colour while the surface changed at once, and for that window the pairing could fall below 4.5:1. This covers every way the mode can change: a change of the `mode` prop and, while no `mode` is pinned, a change of `<html>`'s `data-theme` (a host page's own theme toggle) or of the OS colour scheme. Transitions run as before from the next frame on, and `prefers-reduced-motion` is unchanged. A nested `ThemeScope` is left alone, because its colours never follow its parent's mode. The scope marks itself with a `data-theme-changing` attribute for that frame; nothing is injected into the document, so a strict `style-src` is not involved. The rule is an `@scope` block, so a browser without `@scope` support (before Chrome 118, Safari 17.4 or Firefox 146) ignores it and keeps the old fade.
