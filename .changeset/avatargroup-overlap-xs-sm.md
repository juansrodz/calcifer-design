`AvatarGroup` at `xs` and `sm` no longer cuts the end of the second letter of each avatar's initials. The stack overlapped by two space steps (8 px) at every size, which at `xs` (24 px avatars) and `sm` (28 px) put the next avatar's edge, and the ring round it, across the letters: up to 4.5 px of the initials' box at `xs` and 2.5 px at `sm`, and under forced colours the cut letter ran into the neighbour's outline. Those two sizes now overlap less: half a space step (2 px) at `xs` and one step (4 px) at `sm`. In Arial and Helvetica widths, the initials of the stories' names now clear the neighbour's edge by 1.4 px at both sizes (they were 4.5 px and 2.5 px under it), and no pixel of them is covered in light, dark or forced colours. With the system font, or any face wider than those two, the ring can still shave up to about 1.2 px off the last stem of the second letter at `xs` and `sm`, against 3.8 to 5.8 px before. `md`, `lg` and `xl` are unchanged at two steps.

This is a normal-mode change, on purpose, and `sm` is the default size, so every group with no `size` prop changes: each avatar after the first sits 4 px further out (6 px at `xs`), and a five-item row is 16 px wider at `sm` (24 px at `xs`). The overlap follows the group's `size`, not the size the avatars are drawn at, so a group sized through `--avatar-size` and `--avatar-font-size` keeps the overlap of its `size` (4 px at the default); there is no hook for the overlap. The stack still overlaps, at 8% (`xs`) and 14% (`sm`) of an avatar's width, close to `lg`'s 12% and `xl`'s 8%. A pair of the widest capitals (`WW`, `MW`) can still run under the ring at these sizes, as it can at `md`; only an overlap near zero would clear those.

New `AvatarGroup` story `Photos`: a group of photo avatars drawn from inlined data (no network), light and dark portraits side by side, so the forced-colours edge between two photos can be looked at.
