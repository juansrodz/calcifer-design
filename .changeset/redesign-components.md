'@calcifer-design/ui': minor
---

Add eight components: `Collapsible`, `TabBar`, `EmptyState`, `AvatarGroup`, `ChoiceCard`, `ToggleChip` with `ToggleChipGroup`, `ProgressBar` and `SearchField`. Existing components gain slots and sizes. `Avatar` takes `colorSeed`, the `xs` and `xl` sizes, the `--avatar-size` and `--avatar-font-size` size hooks and `className`, and `avatarColorIndex` is exported beside it. `Button` and `LinkButton` take `tone`, `badge` and `size="lg"`; `IconButton` takes the `soft` variant; `Segmented` takes `badge`, `hideLabel` and `fill`. `Dialog` takes `headerStart`, `headerEnd`, `headingHidden`, `closeVariant` and `closeHidden`, and its bottom and end sheets pin their footer. `NavMenu` takes a generic item type and `orientation`; `TextInput` and `TimePicker` take `startIcon`; `DatePicker` takes `formatOptions` and `iconPlacement`. Eight consumer hooks, each defaulting to the look it replaces, let an app restyle a part from a class on the component or on a wrapper of its own: `--toggle-chip-pressed-ring`, `--icon-button-shadow`, `--segmented-pressed-shadow`, `--dialog-heading-size`, `--dialog-heading-tracking`, `--dialog-heading-leading`, `--sheet-handle-width` and `--sheet-handle-height`. One look change comes with them: bottom sheets gain a grab handle (L2-12).

`Button` gains `variant="row"` (full width, start-aligned, no fill at rest, a 44 px target) and `endIcon`, which `LinkButton` takes too; a `startIcon` slot; and `pending`, which shows a spinner in place of the leading icon, keeps the label, sets `aria-busy` and `aria-disabled` and blocks activation while the button stays focusable. `Skeleton` reads `--skeleton-radius` on its text and block shapes. Every default renders as before.

The full hook list is fourteen: the eight above, the two `Avatar` size hooks, `--skeleton-radius`, `--empty-state-heading-size`, `--collapsible-heading-size` and `--progress-bar-track-size`. The widened unions (`ButtonVariant` gains `row`, `ButtonSize` gains `lg`, `IconButtonVariant` gains `soft`, `AvatarSize` gains `xs` and `xl`) make an exhaustive `switch` over them stop compiling until it handles the new members. `pending` sits beside `loading`, which is unchanged; a button that is both `disabled` and `pending` keeps the disabled look and stays focusable and `aria-busy`. Without a `startIcon`, a pending button hides its badge and end icon by opacity, so its size holds. `Button` and `LinkButton` always write `data-tone="accent"` when no tone is given, and their accent-scoped hover rules gain one attribute of specificity. A selected secondary button has a transparent border, and in forced colours a selected primary or secondary button draws a `Highlight` edge, which wins on weight.

`Dialog`'s close control is a 36 px circle (`--size-close`, new in tokens 0.3.0) with an invisible 44 px hit area, inside a new end group in the header, so the header's DOM changes. The header row centres its children; a `headerEnd` of `null`, `false` or `''` with `closeHidden` renders no empty end group. A sheet's grab handle is 40 by 5 px in `--color-sheet-handle`. In a sheet the body alone scrolls, and the header, description and footer no longer shrink. The body gains room for focus rings (a padding with an equal negative margin), so its scroll clip sits about 4 px further out. Opening focus goes to the close control, or to the popup on a touch open; with `closeHidden` it is Base UI's default.

`NavMenu`'s wide list writes `data-orientation`, its items are `readonly`, its vertical form keeps the links at the top, and a new `labels` prop (`{ open, close }`) can replace the menu button's default "Open menu" and "Close menu". `Alert` and `ErrorBoundary` take `headingLevel={1}` for a full-page error. `ProgressBar`'s `statusLabels` gains `indeterminate`, and a `helperText` of `''` or `true` renders no helper and no `aria-describedby`. A chosen `Segmented` option draws its badge in the inverse colours. `ToggleChipGroup` works inside a `Form`: an explicit error blocks submit and takes focus, and its values are listed in the `Form` docs. In single mode Tab lands on the pressed chip, `name` is no longer written on the fieldset, and an invalid group's unpressed chips draw the danger ring. `SearchField` registers with a `Form` (the values include its name, and nothing while it is disabled); its clear control's hover is `--color-surface-3`, the `sm` focus ring is drawn inward, the field shows a text cursor, and a disabled input shows `not-allowed`.

`DatePicker` falls back to `{ dateStyle: 'medium' }` when `Intl` refuses `formatOptions`, with one development warning per picker, and it reads `process.env` only after `Intl` has refused. A leading icon in `DatePicker`, `TextInput` or `TimePicker` is followed by a `--space-2` gap, so `iconPlacement="start"` moves the value 4 px. With `startIcon`, `TimePicker` hides the native picker button and its icon opens the picker instead; the icon shows a pointer cursor except when disabled, and `showPicker`'s `SecurityError`, `NotAllowedError` and `InvalidStateError` are swallowed.

`TabBar` is lifted by `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, which needs `viewport-fit=cover`; in forced colours its pill gains 2 px and the current link draws a `Highlight` outline. `ChoiceCard` draws its 1 px border only in forced colours, where a disabled card is `GrayText` without extra dimming. `Collapsible`'s chevron is muted; a disabled one dims its contents rather than the button; its aside is wrapped in a span, so plain-text asides dim with the label when disabled, and a `null`, `false` or `''` aside adds no space. `EmptyState`'s illustration, children and actions and `ChoiceCard`'s description and icon now draw `0` and `NaN`.

`Avatar`'s initials skip leading punctuation and words that are punctuation alone ("Juan –" gives "J"), each word gives its first letter or digit ("Dad (Pablo)" gives "DP"), and a name of nothing but punctuation keeps its marks.

`SkipLink` reads the inverse pair, `--color-inverse` and `--color-inverse-text`, instead of the accent pair, so under the house theme it is a dark pill in light mode. `base.css` now sets `margin: 0` on `h4`, `h5` and `h6` across the page.
