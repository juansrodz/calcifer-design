`DataGrid`: no row of body text shows above the sticky header band any more. In a GPU-composited Chrome (an ordinary headed window) the compositor could place the sticky band up to a device pixel below the scroll area's top edge, so while the body was scrolled a one-pixel slice of the rows under it showed between the card header and the band. The band now sticks 1px above that edge, and its top hairline is the scroll area's own top border, which stays in place while the band slides under it. At rest the grid sits 1px lower than before (the border adds a pixel above the band); while scrolled, the stuck band shows 1px less of its own top padding. The top hairline now lies on the card's surface rather than the band's, so it reads lighter. In headed Chrome at a device pixel ratio of 1, when the scroll area's top falls at a fractional offset, it can also spread over two device rows at rest, softer still, and sits on one row once the band sticks. A row scrolled up to the band, as ArrowUp and PageUp do and as the browser does for a focused cell, now lands one pixel higher than the band's height: its top line sits right under the band's hairline, with no gap between them, and a focused cell's focus ring stays whole below the band.
